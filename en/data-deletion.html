Fino — Account & Data Deletion

App: Fino ｜ Android package: com.fino.android ｜ iOS bundle ID: com.fino.iosApp
Last updated: 29 July 2026 ｜ 切換至繁體中文

This page explains how to request deletion of your Fino account and associated data, what gets deleted, what may be retained, and how long it takes. For details on what data Fino collects and how it is used, see the Privacy Policy.

1. How to request account deletion

Please note: Fino does not currently provide an in-app "delete account" button. The only available way to request deletion today is to email the developer. (The developer is aware of this gap and plans to add an in-app deletion entry point in a future release.)

Email: liokhgf@gmail.com

Suggested subject: Fino 帳號刪除申請 (or Fino Account Deletion Request)

Please include:

  • The email address you used to register with Fino (the email behind your Google or Apple sign-in).
  • Which platform you use (Android or iOS) — this helps speed up verification.
  • If you only want part of your data deleted (for example only transaction photos), please say so.

After receiving your request, the developer will first verify your identity — for example by confirming that the request comes from the same email address registered to the account, and replying to ask for additional information if needed. Deletion is performed only after verification, so that nobody else can delete your data on your behalf.

2. Data that will be deleted

Once your identity is verified, the following cloud data tied to your account (Firebase Auth uid) is deleted:

  • Your Firebase Auth account, including the uid, email address, avatar URL and sign-in provider (Google / Apple).
  • Everything in your cloud space, including:
    • All transaction fields (amount, original currency, exchange rate, income/expense type, category, date, notes you wrote yourself, account, travel mode, source marker)
    • Categories
    • Budget amounts
    • Recurring transaction rules
    • Quick-entry templates
    • Settings such as your primary currency
  • Transaction photos in Cloud Storage — photos you manually attached to transactions.
  • Push notification (FCM) tokens — you will no longer receive Fino push notifications.
  • Your personal shared-ledger side data — the list of ledgers you belong to and the private personal categories and rate snapshots you assigned to shared line items.

For clarity: receipt images captured or selected for AI receipt scanning, along with OCR text recognition and AI inference, are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded to the cloud. There is therefore no cloud copy of those images or of the inference to delete. To clear the cached receipt images from your device, uninstall the app — the in-app "Reset App" clears local transactions, categories and preferences but does not guarantee removal of cached receipt images.

⚠️ One distinction to keep in mind: the recognition results you confirm and save (item names, amounts) become fields of a transaction, and transactions do sync to the cloud once you are signed in. That text is therefore part of the transaction data listed above and is removed together with your account.

3. Data that may be retained or cannot be fully deleted

To preserve the integrity of other users' shared bookkeeping records and to comply with legal requirements, the following may remain after your account is deleted:

DataWhy it is retainedWhat we do about it
Shared-ledger content you shared with other members (entries, splits, item names, repayment records) These records also belong to the other members of the same ledger and form their joint accounting history; deleting them outright would break their settlement balances. The entries stay in that ledger. We clear your display name and avatar from that ledger's member record; entries, splits and repayments still carry your internal account identifier (uid), because that is the field that determines whose money is whose — removing it would break the other members' balances outright.
Shared-ledger activity history (including previous and new values of changes) This history is append-only by design — the server-side rules explicitly forbid any update or deletion — so members can trace changes and resolve disputes. Entries are retained exactly as written; we can neither edit nor delete them (the developer is bound by the same rules; only an out-of-band admin operation could alter them, and we do not do that). The history itself records only the actor's internal account identifier (uid), the field name and the before/after values — not your display name or email.
The minimum records required by law or for abuse / fraud prevention In limited cases (for example a lawful request from an authority, or handling abuse) retaining certain records is legally necessary. Only what is necessary is kept, it is not used for any other purpose, and it is deleted once no longer needed.

If you would prefer to leave your shared ledgers before deleting your account, or want to coordinate with the other members about the ledger content, mention it in your email and the developer will help.

4. Timeline and retention period

  • Timeline: deletion is completed within 30 days of receiving your request (identity verification included in that window), and you will receive a confirmation reply.
  • Backup residue: after deletion, short-lived residual copies may still exist in the backend provider's (Firebase) backup and logging systems. These residual copies are cleared within a reasonable period as part of the routine backup cycle and are no longer used to provide the service.
  • Fino keeps no additional copies of personal data for marketing or analytics — the app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

5. If you only want to delete local data on your device

If you do not want to delete your account and only want to clear the data on this device, you can use the "Reset App" function inside the app, which clears your local transactions, categories and preferences.

Note that "Reset App" has a limited scope: it does not clear shared-ledger local data or downloaded AI model files, and it does not guarantee clearing cached receipt images. To remove everything Fino keeps on the device, uninstall the app.

Important warning: "Reset App" is not the same as deleting your account. As long as your account still exists, signing back in with the same account will sync your cloud data (Firestore / Cloud Storage transactions, categories, budgets and photos) back onto the device. To actually delete your cloud data, submit an account deletion request by email as described in section 1.

6. Contact

For any question about data deletion, email liokhgf@gmail.com. Fino is built and maintained by an individual developer, who will reply as soon as possible.
