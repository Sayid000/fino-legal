AI receipt scanning

Why do I have to download such a large file?

Because recognition runs entirely on your phone, the model has to live on your device. Receipt photos are never uploaded to any server — that is a deliberate design choice, and this one-time download is its cost. Once downloaded, it works offline.

Will it work on my phone?

On iPhone you need an iPhone 12 or later running iOS 17 or later. If your iPhone supports Apple Intelligence, Fino uses the built-in system model and no download is needed.

On Android it depends on memory: the smaller model needs 3 GB or more, the more accurate one needs 6 GB or more. Devices that don't qualify can still use every other feature of Fino — only AI photo capture is unavailable.

The recognition result is wrong. What now?

Results are always shown for you to confirm before anything is saved, and you can edit the merchant, amount and every line item. Creases, glare, small print and faint thermal printing all hurt accuracy — flattening the receipt and using even lighting helps a lot.

Voice input

How do I use it?

Tap the microphone in the app and say something like "lunch 120". On iPhone you can also say "Record with Fino" to Siri, and Siri will ask what you want to record.

Is my audio uploaded?

iPhone: no. Fino forces on-device recognition and will not start if the device doesn't support it.

Android: on-device is preferred, but it cannot be guaranteed. When your device has no local model for the language, the system may fall back to Google's servers — and it does not tell us when it does. If you don't want your audio to have any chance of leaving the device, use manual entry or receipt scanning instead; neither involves voice at all. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Sync and accounts

Do I have to sign in?

No. Every core feature of Fino works without signing in, with your data stored on your phone. Signing in only enables cloud sync (so your data survives a phone change) and shared ledgers.

I got a new phone. How do I move my data?

Sign in on the old phone and sync once, then sign in with the same account on the new phone. If you have never signed in, your data exists only on the old device — sign in and sync before switching.

What happens if I sign in with a different account on the same phone?

Fino clears the local data and replaces it with the new account's data, so that the previous account's records cannot leak into the new one. The old account's data remains intact in its own cloud storage and comes back when you sign in again.

Shared ledgers

Can someone on a different phone system join?

Yes. When anyone records an entry, it appears on every member's screen immediately — no pull-to-refresh needed.

What can other members see?

Only what you record in that ledger: display name, avatar, amounts, item names and entry notes. Your personal categories and personal ledger are never visible to them.

Your data

I want to delete my account

In the app: Settings tab → scroll to the bottom → Delete Account. It runs immediately after identity confirmation, usually within seconds. Full details in Account and Data Deletion.

I only want to clear the data on this device

Uninstall Fino from the device. This does not affect your data in the cloud.

Does Fino have ads or subscriptions?

No. Fino is free — no subscriptions, no in-app purchases, no ads, and no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs of any kind.

Contact

Bug reports, feature requests and privacy-related requests all go to liokhgf@gmail.com.

When reporting a problem, including your device model, OS version and Fino version (at the bottom of the Settings tab) plus the steps that led to it helps enormously.
